    Advanced

    Camera connection

    Use plain HTTP on port 8080 — HTTPS is about 30% slower because the camera's processor does the encryption, and it adds nothing on a local network (§3.1).

    Find camera probes x.x.x.1–254 on ports 8080 and 80. A camera that hasn't allowed this page (CORS, below) looks exactly like an empty address.

    The camera must be told to accept this page's origin, and it forgets after a power cycle. python serve.py --camera <ip> keeps it set for you. The write payload isn't documented, so this tries the plausible variants and shows exactly what the camera replied.

    Upload API

    Resolved endpoint base: —. /api and https:// are added automatically: root paths hit the site's frontend (405), and an http:// address redirects POST into GET (§4). Enter /backend as the site address to route uploads through this server's proxy (python serve.py --backend <url>); it avoids needing CORS grants from the site and the bucket. To upload directly instead, add to the site's WATERMARK_APP_ORIGINS and to the bucket's CORS rules.

    Without the proxy, the bucket needs CORS allowing PUT from with the Content-Type header.

    How to use the Camera Companion

    2. 2
      Photograph a partAs many angles as the listing needs.
    3. 3
      Last photo: the SKU labelThat tells the app which part the photos belong to. They're then watermarked and sent to Inventory as a draft.

    Getting the label read first time

    • Fill most of the frame with the label and keep it in focus.
    • Keep the label flat and avoid glare from lights.
    • One part at a time — shoot its label before starting the next part.

    If something goes wrong

    "Camera not reachable"
    Check the camera is on and its Wi-Fi is connected, and that this device is on the same Wi-Fi. If the camera went to sleep, half-press the shutter.
    "Photos need a SKU"
    The label wasn't read — not even its printed number. Open Photos → Needs SKU, select that part's photos, choose Assign SKU, type the 7-digit number, then Upload.
    "Didn't reach Inventory"
    Press Retry. Nothing is lost — photos stay on this device until Inventory has them. If it keeps failing, check the internet connection, then Settings → Inventory uploads → Test.
