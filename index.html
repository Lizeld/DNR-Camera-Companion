Camera

Use HTTP, port 8080. HTTPS costs about 30% throughput because the camera's SoC is the bottleneck when encrypting, and TLS buys nothing on a LAN against a self-signed cert (§3.1).

Probes x.x.x.1–254 on ports 8080 and 80. Only positive results are meaningful: a host that is not a CORS-enabled camera fails the same way an empty address does.

CORS is what makes a browser client viable at all. The write payload shape is not documented, so this tries the plausible variants and shows exactly what the camera replied.
